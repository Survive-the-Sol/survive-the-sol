# Survive the Sol – 7-slide deck

## 1 · Problem
- Kids love space, but they never see the hard choices astronauts make with limited **power, air and protection**.
- Solar storms are real and dangerous, yet invisible.
- Most kids' space games are shooting games or quizzes.

## 2 · Solution
- **Survive the Sol**: a 3D trainer for ages 7–12. Keep your Moon crew safe for 12 days.
- Pick **Emergency Cards**, explore, and use the right card when a **real NASA solar storm** hits.
- Runs in any browser: no login, no install, no weapons.

## 3 · Gameplay (one day)
1. Pick 3 of 5 Emergency Cards. Each one has a cost.
2. Land and collect supplies for Power, Life Support and Radiation Safety.
3. Danger hits. Use a card within 5 seconds: right card = full effect, wrong card = half, no card = full damage.
4. Run into the safe zone around the base.
5. Night report: stars, bars before and after, a NASA fact.

## 4 · Real NASA data
- **DONKI:** every storm is a real flare, for example "X8.7 · 14 May 2024".
- **RadLab:** radiation is 1.94× the ISS level near the Moon and 1.36× on Mars, and storm damage uses these numbers.
- **OSDR:** verified space-biology facts (EDEN ISS greenhouse, ISS tomatoes, Veggie lettuce, BioSentinel).
- **Mars Trek:** the real Viking mosaic on the Mars ground.
- Everything is labelled "archived NASA data".

## 5 · What kids learn
- **Power budget:** every action costs energy.
- **Radiation shielding:** the right shield at the right time.
- **Life support:** air, water and food never stop running out.
- **Mars comm delay:** Earth replies about 20 minutes late, so you decide alone.

## 6 · Impact
- Classroom-ready: a 3-day demo mode and Teacher mode; works on laptops and phones.
- Replayable: random real flares, dangers and card offers every day.
- Kids take home their own "The Moon Times" front page.
