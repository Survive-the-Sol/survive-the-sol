# Survive the Sol: Storm Royale – 7-slide deck

## 1 · The problem
- Kids love space, but the real job of an astronaut is invisible to them: making hard choices with **limited power, air and protection**.
- Space weather is real and dangerous: solar flares can send dangerous radiation into space.
- Most space games for kids are either shooting games or simple quizzes.

## 2 · Our solution
- **Survive the Sol: Storm Royale**: a 3D "storm royale" game for ages 7–12.
- Drop onto the Moon or Mars, collect supplies, drive a rover, and reach shelter before a **real NASA solar storm** hits.
- **No weapons, no violence.** The other crews are friends you race, not enemies.
- Runs in any browser: no login, no install.

## 3 · Gameplay (one sol = one round)
1. **Plan:** choose 1 of 3 mission cards. Every card helps one bar and costs another.
2. **Drop:** jump from the lander and steer with thrusters.
3. **Explore:** collect supplies with rarity levels; the backpack has limited slots; the rover is fast but uses Power.
4. **Storm:** the safe zone shrinks toward the shelter.
5. **Night report:** results, the crew race, XP and a NASA fact.
- 12 sols on the Moon unlock Mars: dust storms, stronger flares, a 20-minute Earth reply delay.

## 4 · Real NASA data
- **DONKI:** every storm is a real solar flare (for example "X8.7 · 14 May 2024"). The class sets the storm's speed and strength.
- **RadLab:** measured radiation is 1.94× the ISS level near the Moon and 1.36× on the Mars surface. The game uses these numbers.
- **OSDR:** 8 verified space-biology facts (EDEN ISS greenhouse, ISS tomatoes, Veggie lettuce, BioSentinel).
- **Mars Trek:** the real Viking colour mosaic on the Mars ground.
- Everything is shown on screen as "archived NASA data" and never as live data.

## 5 · What kids learn
- **Power budget:** every action costs energy.
- **Radiation shielding:** watch the storm and get to shelter early; shields help.
- **Life support & food:** air, water and food run out; grow and recycle.
- **Communication delay:** on Mars, Earth's help arrives about 20 minutes late, so you decide.
- Taught by the ROVI tutorial, live tips, and an end-of-mission learning summary with a "What would a real astronaut do?" debrief.

## 6 · Impact
- Classroom-ready: a 3-sol demo mode, teacher mode, works on school laptops and phones.
- Replayable: random real flares, loot, card offers and bot behaviour each time.
- Kids take home their own **newspaper front page** (PNG) with their name and rank.
- Accessible: large text, keyboard play, pause, reduce motion.
